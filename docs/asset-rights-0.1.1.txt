ECZOS 0.1.1 asset-rights declaration
====================================

Status: approved-for-eczos-0.1.1
Record date: 2026-09-27

This release continues to use the ECZOS logos, wallpapers, boot animations,
OOBE video and OOBE music covered by the ECZOS 0.1.0 declaration. The project
owner previously confirmed that those original assets were created for
EasyComp Zeeland and may be redistributed in official ECZOS releases.

The product screenshots refreshed for ECZOS 0.1.1 were captured from the
project-owned ECZOS GamePC on 2026-09-27. They contain ECZOS and third-party
application interfaces only, with no personal files, credentials or private
messages. Third-party names and interface elements remain the property of
their respective owners and are shown solely to describe interoperability and
included functionality.

This record approves those assets for redistribution, unmodified, in official
ECZOS 0.1.1 packages, installation images, recovery media and release pages.
